Humans:

Members of this race are settled all over the world and make up the bulk of the population. Humanity is the only race whose members can be gifted at birth with one of two elements. Because of the differences in magic, internal conflicts and waves of discrimination along elemental lines flare up again and again in many cities. In some settlements the ruling councils went as far as madness, wiping out entire families with the wrong element. This enmity is still strong to this day, which is why it is extremely important for the council to check carefully before granting a trade licence, so as to avoid internal clashes on political grounds. In Eldhold, however, there is relative calm on this matter. Probably because the people here already have someone to hate. Most often the inborn element passes to the child from the mother, so women with a predisposition to unique elemental traits are highly valued in society. In rare cases, though, the father's genes can suppress the mother's blood and change the infant's element. If the father's element differs from the mother's, such a mixing can prove fatal for the mother and lead to a dangerous elemental anomaly in the newborn. Such cases are extremely rare, but the odds are still not zero.

Zorlings:

Zorlings! that is exactly what these creatures call themselves in their native tongue. For a long time they lived in isolation in the wastes after the great cataclysm. Under the influence of little-studied anomalies they are thought to have gradually begun to gain reason. About three hundred years ago merchant caravans started noticing monsters that were non-aggressive and, at first glance, thinking. That is how the rumour of beastfolk began. At first traders caught lone specimens for experiments and study. In the three hundred years since they were discovered, the Zorlings have managed to take root in only four known human settlements, and in each of them they are, to put it mildly, unwelcome. Despite the hostility everywhere, their communities have switched almost entirely to the human language. It turned out to be far more convenient for everyday communication because of its simplified structure and its written form, although the Zorlings themselves use writing quite rarely. Despite the hatred and persecution, They go on surviving in groups inside the cities, since that is better in any case than returning to the deadly Wastes beyond the straal walls. Zorlings are creatures covered in thick fur, with pointed ears and an elongated muzzle. Instead of hands they have four-fingered paws, and on their legs coarser five-toed ones. Such an anatomy lets them move on both two and four limbs. In everyday life they mostly walk on their legs. In battle, however, thanks to their animal build, Zorlings often drop onto all fours, which lets them reach enormous speed and make swift lunges, tearing their prey apart in close combat.

Monsters:

Entities about whose true origin nothing is known. Among the inhabitants of human settlements, however, there is a grim hypothesis that monsters are the descendants of those people who did not manage to take shelter behind the settlement walls during the great cataclysm, so that the anomalies of the wastes twisted their bodies and minds beyond recognition. In themselves these beasts are not intelligent, and at times are mad, so if you see yet another klork jumping into a pit, don't be surprised. Even so, superstitions still live among the people that the world is vast, and that somewhere in the unexplored depths of the wastes there lives someone else who, like the Zorlings, has managed to develop intellect. Hundreds of species of monsters live in the boundless lands, and humanity does not even suspect that some of them exist. For some unknown reason most monsters are extremely aggressive they not only storm merchant caravans, but also attack each other regularly. For the most part they pose no colossal threat to prepared travellers, and some specimens, because of their hideous deformations, look like pitiful cripples, doomed by the anomalies from the start to an agonising death by starvation. The overwhelming majority of monsters are endowed with the little-studied element of stasis, though unique specimens with void or electric powers do turn up now and then. In all the years of research, scholars and travellers have failed to find a single monster that was endowed with the element of the sun. But who knows, perhaps humanity simply still knows too little about the world beyond the walls.
