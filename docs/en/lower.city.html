Lower City:

This district of Eldhold, sprawling along the left bank of the river Eilryos, looks more like slums lying in ruins. Residential blocks occupied by criminal groups are scattered across its whole territory. After the collapse of the once mighty Urum syndicate, the war for territory and resources between the newly formed gangs never dies down. The main form of trade here is barter, though the richest crime bosses sometimes settle accounts in V-rlings. The air in some parts of the Lower City is stifling and poisonous. Because of the large-scale destruction of the underground laboratories, toxic waste still poisons the earth and the atmosphere, a reminder of the days of mass production of the dangerous stimulant — Euthonate. Rare farming fields and farms have survived here, but during the suppression of Urum most of the farms and crops were destroyed or hopelessly contaminated with chemicals. The Lower City has no clear laws and no single authority; the main rule of survival here is not to draw the attention of the council and of the influential bosses, so as to avoid a swift reprisal. Despite the difficult relations between the cities, the council keeps sending provisions into the slums, though this help falls catastrophically short for everyone .
