Elements:

Hi! Elements are a mechanic that is not from vanilla D&D, but I hope you like it and it will not kill the fun. Have a good game!

So, above you can see a picture with the 4 elements. The arrows between them show which element has the upper hand. Fire suppresses Stasis, Stasis suppresses Void, Void suppresses Electricity, and Electricity closes the cycle by suppressing Fire. The direction of the arrows shows the dominance of one element over another. The dominant type of magic deals +1 to the final damage for each die. The attacker takes less damage from the element that is vulnerable to them, and the maximum result on the enemy's damage die is lowered by 1 (a rolled 4 counts as 3, and a 6 counts as 5). This vulnerability, however, mostly affects pure damage. (status effects and anomalies that are applied may ignore this mechanic.)
