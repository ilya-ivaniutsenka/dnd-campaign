Eilryos:

A majestic river that grants life to all settlements such as Eldorhold. In none of the cities does anyone know where its source lies or where its waters go. Yet without the Eilryos humanity would not have had a single chance of survival - the river supplies people not only with fresh water, but also provides them with electricity.

Eldhold:

One of the settlements lying along the river Eilryos. Most of the local settlements live in peace and trade various crops, livestock and craft goods with one another. Such an exchange is extremely profitable for both sides. Eldhold, however, was a little different: the river divided it into two separate cities with no common seat of government. In the end this led to catastrophe — on the orders of the Council the left side completely cleared out the right bank.

The Urum Syndicate:

Once a great criminal organisation of the Lower City, which fully controlled the right bank of Eldhold. Their ideological views and goals diverged radically from the policy of the official authorities. This constant confrontation often spilled over into open clashes between the two banks and in the end led to a bloody denouement. The syndicate's main source of income and influence was the mass production of the dangerous stimulant — Euthonate, which the gangsters brewed by the gallon and planned to supply to other settlements all across the wastes. Urum's last and most audacious stunt was a large-scale attempt at sabotage, aimed at blowing up the protective grid by the river in Eldhold. If not for the heroic deed of the Hero Eloy, the criminals would have managed to destroy this strategic hub and take the breached positions. Such a triumph would have given the group total dominance over the Council and opened up boundless opportunities for an unchecked drug trade. Thanks to the Hero Eloy, however, the attack was foiled, and in response the Council immediately organised a total clearance of the right bank, which destroyed Urum's monopoly for good and put an end to the history of this once mighty faction.

The Council:

The central organ of organised power in the settlements, coordinating most internal and external processes. Within each particular place this body is called simply the Council, but when the authorities of other settlements are mentioned it is customary to add a geographical name — the government of Eldhold, for example, is called the Eldhold Council outside its borders. Ordinary citizens do not know for certain who exactly belongs to this structure and what the mechanism for selecting new members is. Among the population there is a belief that membership of the Council cannot be inherited or bought, and that seats there are handed out exclusively by invitation, for outstanding achievements before society. The Council's duties are extensive and directly affect people's survival in the wastes. It is this body that issues the official merchant licences which give traders the legal right to travel through dangerous lands and do business with the other settlements scattered across the wastes. The Council fully controls the recruitment, training and assignment of guardsmen, and also takes charge of the affairs of the Lower City: from organising regular patrols and deliveries of humanitarian provisions to making key internal decisions and settling local conflicts.

Eldor:

A great man of antiquity. Although ordinary people can possess only one element, the ancient scriptures claim that he was endowed with two elements at once - Sun and Void. Practically nothing is known about Eldor himself, but legends passed from mouth to mouth have come down to our days, saying that he was one of those who raised the Straal Walls. These are of course only rumours, since people are inclined to credit historical figures with incredible feats. The only confirmed facts about him are his part in the Council and that a city was named in his honour. But neither who exactly gave the city this name nor the true reasons for that decision have been uncovered to this day. All that is known is that after Eldor one of the Verigas remained in this world.

Eloy:

the hero of this century, whose name entered history forever after he saved the Upper City from a catastrophic terrorist attack at the cost of his own life. Biography: Eloy was a twenty-year-old lad who had to live through no small number of misfortunes. When he turned fifteen, his entire family died. Left alone, the young man decided to enrol in the academy for guardsmen. In the course of his training he not only mastered the martial arts to perfection, but also discovered in himself a colossal inborn potential for the Sun element. For outstanding service the Council rewarded the distinguished guardsman with the «Stardust Pistol» — one of the few Verigas the council owned. Eloy mastered this most difficult artefact with astonishing ease, for which he was granted the official title of Hero of Eldhold. The deed and the death: The sabotage began under cover of an illegal delivery of Euthonate to the port. The perpetrators planned it cunningly: they filled only the first eight barrels on the wagon with the banned stimulant, while the other twenty-four concealed a powerful explosive. By the organisers' design, the contraband was meant to be discovered deliberately at the entrance. The keepers of order, deciding that this was yet another routine interception of euthonate, sent the whole cargo to the station for inspection. There a double agent was already waiting for the cart, ready to trigger the detonator. On that fateful day Eloy was inside the station building. Being vigilant, he noticed one of the guards rummaging suspiciously in the barrels and then trying to slip away abruptly and unnoticed. Suspecting something was wrong, the guardsman rushed to inspect the cargo himself and found the explosives. There was no time to panic: Eloy instantly sealed the barrels, loaded them back onto the wagon and tore off towards the exit at a furious speed, trying to get the deadly cargo beyond the Straal Wall. He did not manage to get to a safe distance and died at the epicentre of the terrible explosion. That desperate act, however, saved the city from the irreparable. Had the detonation happened inside the station, the blast wave would not only have taken hundreds of lives, but would also have completely destroyed the protective grid. Such a breach would have opened the perfect opportunity for the criminal group Urum taking the breached position, the gangsters would have gained total superiority over the Council's forces, irreversibly changing the balance of power. When Eloy died his body was never found, and the legendary Veriga was lost along with him.
