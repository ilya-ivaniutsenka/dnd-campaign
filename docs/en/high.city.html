Upper City:

A city of technology where people know neither hunger nor shortage, lying in the land of Eldhold, on the left side of the river Eilryos. The city is surrounded by the Straal Walls and joined by bridges to the right bank of the anarchic city. The Upper City has water trade routes and air ports for communication and exchange with other settlements. The local currency, V-rlings, is in demand in other lands too. The guardsmen who protect the city are trained in combat and well equipped, and they are not averse to using their strength on the orders of the Council. The people of this city fear and hate the beastfolk, since it is commonly held that they are not very bright, and at times dangerous to society. The main elements of the locals here are sun and void, though who knows what hides behind the masks of some of its residents.
